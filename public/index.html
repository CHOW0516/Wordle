    How to play
    • Everyone gets their own secret word and 6 guesses. Green means right letter, right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word, but in another spot.
    • Solve a word to score points (6 for 1 guess, down to 1 for 6 guesses). Then you get a new word right away.
    • Each solve earns a sabotage. Click a player to send it. Solving in 3 guesses or fewer earns a stronger one.
    • Fog hides colors, Key lock blocks 3 letters, Scramble shuffles the keyboard, Freeze stops typing, Lost row takes away a guess.
    • The match lasts 5 minutes. Most points wins.
