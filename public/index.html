    How to play
    • Everyone gets their own secret word and 6 guesses. Green means right letter, right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word, but in another spot.
    • Solve a word to score points (6 for 1 guess, down to 1 for 6 guesses). Then you get a new word right away.
    • You can watch your opponents' boards. You see their colors, not their letters.
    • Each solve earns a sabotage. Tap an opponent's board to send it. Solving in 3 guesses or fewer earns a strong one.
    • Strong: Lost row (one guess less), Key lock (3 letters blocked), Steal (take 2 points), Word swap (they get a new word).
    • Weak: Fog (colors hidden), Scramble (keyboard shuffled), Freeze (no typing), Amnesia (old letters hidden), Invisible ink (typed letters hidden), Hint thief (take a hint).
    • You have 3 hints per match. Each hint shows one letter in its right spot and costs 1 point.
    • The host picks a match length of 3, 5 or 10 minutes. Most points wins.
